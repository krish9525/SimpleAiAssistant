# Corporate Group Health Insurance and Medical Benefits Policy

## 1. Overview and Intent
Our organization prioritizes the health, safety, and physical well-being of our workforce and their families. This Medical and Health Insurance Policy outlines the comprehensive medical benefits, group health insurance coverage (GMC), group personal accident insurance (GPA), outpatient department (OPD) wellness allowances, and hospitalization claim protocols provided to full-time employees from day one of employment.

## 2. Group Medical Cover (GMC) - Inpatient Hospitalization
Every eligible employee is enrolled in our corporate Group Medical Cover policy administered by our designated insurance partner and Third-Party Administrator (TPA):
- Sum Insured and Family Definition: Each employee receives a floater health insurance coverage of INR 5,00,000 (Five Lakh Rupees) annually. The base policy covers the employee, legally wedded spouse, and up to two dependent children (1+3 floater structure). Employees can optionally enroll dependent parents or parents-in-law under a subsidized co-pay parental insurance rider during the annual enrollment window.
- Inpatient Hospitalization Threshold: To qualify for insurance claim settlement, hospitalization must be medically necessary and involve a minimum continuous admission period of twenty-four hours, except for advanced day-care procedures (such as chemotherapy, dialysis, cataract surgery, and minor surgical interventions) where technology permits discharge in less than twenty-four hours.
- Room Rent and ICU Capping: The standard room rent limit is capped at one percent of the total sum insured per day (INR 5,000/day) for normal rooms, and two percent of the sum insured (INR 10,000/day) for Intensive Care Unit (ICU) charges. Choosing accommodation exceeding these financial limits results in proportionate deduction across associated medical billing items.
- Pre-Existing Diseases (PED) and Waiting Periods: Unlike retail health insurance, our corporate group policy waives standard 2-to-4 year waiting periods. Pre-existing diseases, congenital internal conditions, and maternity expenses are covered from the first day of policy activation.
- Maternity and Newborn Coverage: Maternity hospitalization benefits provide up to INR 75,000 for standard normal deliveries and INR 1,00,000 for Caesarean deliveries. Newborn infant coverage is active from day one within the overall family floater sum insured.

## 3. Hospitalization Claim Settlement Procedures
Employees can utilize two distinct claim settlement pathways:
- Cashless Hospitalization: Available across an extensive network of over 8,500 empaneled hospitals nationwide. For planned admissions, employees must present their digital TPA health card and government photo ID at the hospital's insurance desk at least forty-eight hours prior to admission to obtain pre-authorization. For emergency admissions, pre-authorization must be submitted within twenty-four hours of hospitalization.
- Reimbursement Claims: If medical treatment is received at a non-network hospital, the employee must settle hospital bills directly and submit a formal reimbursement claim through the TPA web portal within thirty calendar days of discharge. Mandatory documentation includes original itemized hospital bills, discharge summary, doctor prescription notes, pharmacy invoices, diagnostic test reports, and cancelled bank cheque for NEFT transfer.

## 4. Group Personal Accident (GPA) and Term Life Insurance
In addition to health insurance, the company protects employees against catastrophic unforeseen events:
- Group Personal Accident (GPA): Provides worldwide coverage for accidental death, permanent total disability, and permanent partial disability up to three times the employee's Annual Cost to Company (CTC).
- Group Term Life Insurance (GTL): Offers financial security to designated family nominees in the event of an employee's untimely demise due to any cause, with a lump-sum payout equivalent to two times the annual CTC, subject to minimum underwriting criteria.

## 5. Outpatient Wellness and Preventive Healthcare Benefits
We believe prevention is paramount. The company extends several outpatient wellness benefits:
- Annual Preventive Health Checkup: Employees above the age of twenty-five are eligible for an annual executive health screening package at partnered diagnostic chains at zero personal expense.
- Mental Health and Employee Assistance Program (EAP): Employees and their immediate families have unlimited access to confidential 24/7 psychological counseling, stress management therapy, and mental wellness consultations via our dedicated EAP partner helpline.
- Telemedicine and Doctor Consultations: Unlimited digital video consultations across general medicine, dermatology, nutrition, and pediatrics are available through the corporate health portal.
