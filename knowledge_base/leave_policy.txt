# Comprehensive Employee Leave Policy and Guidelines

## 1. Purpose and Philosophy
The Employee Leave Policy is designed to support the physical, mental, and personal well-being of all full-time employees by providing equitable time off for rest, recreation, health recovery, family responsibilities, and unforeseen emergencies. The organization strongly advocates for work-life harmony and encourages employees to plan their vacations proactively while ensuring uninterrupted project continuity and customer satisfaction.

## 2. Leave Year and Eligibility
The official leave year operates on a standard calendar basis, running from January 1st to December 31st. All permanent, full-time employees are eligible for leave allocations upon their official date of joining. For employees joining midway through the year, leave quotas are prorated based on the remaining completed calendar months. Employees on probation are entitled to accrue casual and sick leaves on a monthly prorated basis, while earned leaves become fully accessible upon probation confirmation.

## 3. Categories of Leave Entitlement
Our organization offers structured leave categories tailored to address various personal, medical, and family circumstances:
- Paid Leave (PL) / Earned Leave (EL): Employees are granted 18 days of Paid Leave per calendar year, accrued at the rate of 1.5 days per completed month of active service. Paid leaves are intended for scheduled vacations, travel, and personal rest. Employees must submit PL requests at least two weeks in advance for leaves exceeding three consecutive days. A maximum of 10 unused PLs can be carried forward to the subsequent calendar year; any excess beyond 10 days will automatically lapse.
- Casual Leave (CL): Employees receive 6 days of Casual Leave annually, credited at the start of the year. CLs are meant for unforeseen personal affairs, domestic exigencies, or urgent social obligations. CLs cannot be combined with Paid Leave and cannot exceed two consecutive days per instance. Unutilized CLs lapse at the end of each calendar year.
- Sick Leave (SL): Employees are allocated 10 days of Sick Leave each calendar year to recover from illness, medical procedures, or injuries. A medical certificate from a registered healthcare professional is mandatory for any sick leave absence extending beyond two consecutive working days. Unused sick leave balances cannot be carried forward or encashed.
- Maternity Leave (ML): Female employees who have completed at least eighty days of continuous service in the twelve months preceding delivery are entitled to 26 weeks (182 calendar days) of fully paid Maternity Leave in compliance with statutory regulations. Up to 8 weeks can be availed before the expected delivery date, with the remaining balance post-childbirth. In case of unfortunate miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy, 6 weeks of paid leave is granted with proper medical certification.
- Paternity Leave: Male employees are entitled to 10 consecutive working days of paid Paternity Leave to support their spouse and welcome a newborn child. Paternity leave must be availed within six months of childbirth and can be taken in a single stretch or two separate tranches of five days each.
- Bereavement Leave: Employees may take up to 5 consecutive paid working days of Bereavement Leave in the unfortunate event of the demise of an immediate family member (spouse, children, parents, siblings, or parents-in-law). Additional personal leave may be requested if extensive travel is required.
- Compensatory Off (Comp-Off): Employees who are explicitly requested by project leadership to work on weekends or declared public holidays for critical deployment activities are eligible for a compensatory day off. Comp-off must be approved by the department manager and utilized within sixty days of accrual, after which it expires.

## 4. Public Holidays and Optional Floater Holidays
The company publishes an annual holiday calendar at the beginning of each year, consisting of 10 mandatory public holidays (such as Independence Day, Republic Day, Mahatma Gandhi Jayanti, Diwali, and Christmas) and 2 optional floater holidays. Employees can select their 2 floater holidays from an approved regional festive list based on their personal cultural traditions.

## 5. Leave Application and Approval Workflow
All leave requests must be logged through the enterprise HRMS portal. Managers are required to approve or address submitted leave requests within forty-eight business hours. In circumstances where an employee takes sudden medical or emergency leave, they must notify their immediate supervisor and HR via email or messaging channel by 10:00 AM on the day of absence and formalize the leave in the HRMS within twenty-four hours of resuming work. Taking leave without formal manager approval or misrepresenting sick leaves constitutes absenteeism and may result in loss of pay or disciplinary action.
